# References

## Editorial references

The structure of this document was informed by two published game-economy whitepapers: an accessible introduction, an explanation of participants and their roles, then economics and project development. Spawn's product decisions and text are its own.

- [NXPC whitepaper](https://docs.nexpace.io/) — example of an abstract, vision, ecosystem, token structure and version history.
- [Axie Infinity whitepaper](https://whitepaper.axieinfinity.com/) — example of introducing a game universe and community before its mechanics. Its landing page identifies the document as last updated in November 2021; it is used as an editorial reference, not evidence of current traction.

These references do not imply affiliation, endorsement or adoption of either project's economic model.
