# Using the token

## One token, different games

Spawn's proposed economy uses one shared token across participating games. The idea is that a player can move between games without setting up a different token balance for each one. Games can still have their own points, scores or items; those do not automatically become withdrawable tokens.

The token name, ticker and supply have not been finalized. The PLAY label in the current website is a demonstration, not the announced token.

## Your wallet and your Spawn balance

Your wallet holds tokens on the blockchain. Your Spawn account would show the amount the platform owes you after deposits, approved purchases, transfers and rewards. Those are two views of where tokens are held, not two separate issuances of the same tokens.

For example, in a future deposit flow, depositing 100 tokens would move 100 tokens from your wallet into platform custody. After confirmation, your Spawn balance would increase by 100. The platform-held tokens back the account balance; this does not create another 100 underlying tokens. This example illustrates accounting only, not a selected deposit minimum or reward rate.

An approved withdrawal would reverse that process: reserve part of your account balance, send the corresponding tokens to your wallet and mark the payment complete. A pending withdrawal must not remain available to spend a second time.

## Spending across games

The intended shared balance could pay for approved game purchases or other supported actions. Every purchase needs a clear price and an explanation of what the player receives. A purchase would reduce the player's available balance and be recorded by the platform.

Whether the payment goes to the creator, a treasury, a reward pool or a defined fee depends on the eventual rules. Those splits have not been chosen. Spending does not necessarily burn tokens, and an in-game item is not necessarily tradeable or an NFT.

## Supply and tokenomics

Tokenomics describes where tokens come from, how they are allocated and what happens as people use them. Spawn has not finalized its maximum supply, minting rights, allocations, vesting, fees or reward budgets.

Before a token launch, the documentation should show those terms, the token and custody addresses, administrative powers and any changes that can be made later. Until then, there is no announced allocation for players, creators, the team or investors.

## What exists today

The current balance and deposit/withdrawal controls are simulated and reset when the page reloads. No real tokens move. There is no live Spawn financial ledger or deposit contract. You can play Rob the Rich's practice build without buying tokens.
